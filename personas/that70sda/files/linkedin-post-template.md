## Production rules

- LinkedIn editor: SINGLE Enters between paragraphs (no blank lines) or the editor multiplies gaps. Verify the text verbatim in the composer before posting; visually inspect the live post after.
- Backlog posts go out in original chronological order, paced like a person (see proposal): one per day max at the start, slot wanders 1-2 hours day to day.
- New posts: same template, dated with that day's date, published the same day as the Substack article.
- No post without its overview (mandatory gate, all channels).
