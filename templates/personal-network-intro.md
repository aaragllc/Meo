# Template - personal-network intro (owner-sent)

The warm-start note: at a persona's launch, the OWNER personally
tells their own network the persona exists. It converts real
relationships into the first subscribers and the first honest
feedback - the two things a cold account cannot buy. One filled
instance per persona lives in its pack at `files/network-intro.md`.

## Hard rules

- **The owner sends it. Personally, by hand, in small batches.**
  Never automated, never mass-mailed by the platform, never posted
  on a persona surface, never sent by the operator on the owner's
  behalf.
- **It bridges identities for one audience only.** The note tells
  people who already know the owner that the persona is theirs.
  That link never appears in public copy, on persona surfaces, or
  anywhere a stranger could find it - the persona's public identity
  protection is untouched.
- **Brief.** 120-180 words for the full version. If it reads like a
  pitch deck, it is wrong.
- **One hero link only.** A menu of links gets no clicks; one
  well-chosen piece gets watched.

## The five beats

1. **Greeting + the arc, one line.** The sender's experience span
   and the eras survived - credibility without a resume.
2. **The name, and what it is.** The persona name stated plainly,
   framed as a writing/recording project, not a brand launch.
3. **Giving back + the explicit "not this".** The mission in one
   sentence (hard-won lessons, shared so others skip the expensive
   version), then the disqualifiers: no guru act, no picks or tips,
   no hype - whatever the persona's equivalents are.
4. **The hero asset.** ONE link: the most representative current
   piece, preferring video (lowest friction for a busy reader),
   with a small time ask ("if you have four minutes"). A flagship
   series piece beats a daily post.
5. **Channels + the feedback ask.** At most 2-3 labeled channel
   links. Then the real point: ask for feedback with three concrete
   prompts - what lands, what's off, what to cover next - and a
   commitment to read every reply. Sign off with the sender's real
   first name; their network knows them by it.

## Slots

| Slot | Filled from |
|---|---|
| `<SENDER_FIRST_NAME>` | the owner, as their network knows them |
| `<ARC_LINE>` | the persona directive's identity section, compressed to one line |
| `<PERSONA_NAME>` | the persona's public name |
| `<MISSION_LINE>` | the directive's core theme ("giving back" wording) |
| `<NOT_LINE>` | the persona's hard bans, phrased warmly |
| `<HERO_TOPIC>` + `<HERO_URL>` + `<HERO_RUNTIME>` | the current flagship piece at send time |
| `<CHANNEL_1..3>` | the persona's live surfaces, labeled (Writing / Podcast / Video) |

## Skeleton

> Hi everyone,
>
> A quick personal note. <ARC_LINE> - I've begun writing and
> recording under the name <PERSONA_NAME>.
>
> There's no <NOT_LINE>. It's simply me giving back: <MISSION_LINE>.
>
> If you have <HERO_RUNTIME>, start here - my piece on
> <HERO_TOPIC>: <HERO_URL>
>
> Everything else lives here:
> <CHANNEL_1>
> <CHANNEL_2>
>
> What I'd honestly value most is your feedback. What lands?
> What's off? What should I cover next? Just reply - I read
> everything.
>
> Thanks,
> <SENDER_FIRST_NAME>

## Short variant (text / DM, 3-4 lines)

> Quick personal share: I've started writing and recording as
> <PERSONA_NAME> - <MISSION_LINE, compressed>. Start with this
> one (<HERO_RUNTIME>): <HERO_URL>. More: <CHANNEL_1>. Honest
> feedback very welcome.

## Cadence and measurement

- Send once at launch, in small personal batches over the first
  days - not one blast.
- Refresh and resend only at a genuine milestone (a series
  completing, a channel launching) and only to people who engaged
  the first time.
- Record sends, replies and attributed subscribers in the launch
  report and the Sunday growth ledger (inventory entry in
  `../SPEC-seo-growth.md`, section 2).
