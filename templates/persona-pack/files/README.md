# files/ - what lives here

Per-persona working documents that are not the directive, the state
snapshot, or skills. Typical contents, all optional and added as the
persona needs them:

- **Channel post templates** - the fixed shape of a post on a channel whose
  format never varies (dated overview, sign-off placement, cross-promo
  footnote). Worked example: that70sda's `linkedin-post-template.md`.
- **Drafts awaiting install** - finished copy that could not be installed
  by automation and waits for a manual step, kept with a note saying where
  it goes and why it is not live yet. Worked example: that70sda's
  `substack-welcome-email-draft.md`.
- **Briefs and prompts for upstream contributors** - the standing asks to
  whoever supplies this persona's content, written so they can be handed
  over unchanged. Worked example: that70sda's
  `stocky-weekly-calendar-prompt.md`.
- **Strategy and roadmap notes** - per-channel plans (a page build-out, a
  launch sequence) that are persona-specific rather than platform-wide.

Rules: files here are persona property - a second persona gets its own
versions, never edits to this persona's. Anything that becomes a rule
belongs in the directive (as a Resolution) or POLICY.md, not only here.
